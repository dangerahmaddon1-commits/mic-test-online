Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains how Mic Test Pro (“we,” “our,” or “the site”) handles information when you visit mic-testpro.vercel.app.

Microphone access and recordings

The microphone test only starts after you activate it and grant permission in your browser. The live input meter and waveform are processed in your browser. If you choose to record a sample, your browser creates an audio object for playback on this page. The page does not upload that sample to a Mic Test Pro server. The sample remains available in the page until you clear it, leave the page, or close the tab; your browser manages its temporary memory. We cannot control other software, extensions, or operating-system services on your device.

You can revoke microphone permission using your browser’s site settings. Stop the test when you finish.
