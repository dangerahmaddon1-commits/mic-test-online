About Mic Test Pro

Mic Test Pro is a simple browser-based tool designed to help people check whether their microphone is available and sending audio to their browser.

What the tool does

  • Requests microphone access through the browser’s standard permission prompt.
  • Shows a live input-level meter and waveform while the microphone is active.
  • Lets visitors record and play back a short sample when their browser supports recording.
  • Provides practical troubleshooting tips for common microphone problems.

How it works

The test uses web browser audio features to access a microphone after the visitor grants permission. The live level and waveform are calculated in the browser. A short sample is made available for playback on the page; this site does not upload that sample to a Mic Test Pro server.

Limitations

A successful browser test does not guarantee that a microphone is configured correctly in every video-call, streaming, or recording application. Results can also vary by browser, device, permissions, and hardware. The meter is an activity indicator, not a calibrated audio measurement.
